Euler’s identity fits five fundamental constants into one line:

$$
e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0
$$

For a circle with radius $r$, the area is $A = \pi r^2$.

## A small probability

The mean of observations $x_1, \ldots, x_n$ is

$$
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{k=1}^{n} x_k
$$
