# Support

Markda is currently a preview release.

When reporting a problem, include:

- your VS Code version and operating system;
- the Markda version;
- the smallest Markdown sample that reproduces the problem;
- the steps you followed and the result you expected; and
- relevant messages from **View → Output** or **Help → Toggle Developer Tools**, with secrets and private content removed.

Open a report in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/tetsuji16/Markda/issues). For Marketplace account or installation issues unrelated to Markda itself, use the Visual Studio Marketplace support link.
